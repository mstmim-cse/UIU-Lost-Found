Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about the UIU Lost & Found platform.

How do I claim an item?

Click the "Claim Item" button on the post. You'll be asked to provide identifying features or proof of ownership. Once the finder reviews and accepts your request, you'll be able to chat and arrange a safe exchange.

What is a Safe Exchange?

We strongly recommend meeting in a public, well-lit area on campus to exchange items, such as the UIU Canteen or the Main Library entrance.

How are users verified?

All users must sign up with a valid `@uiu.ac.bd` email address. This ensures that the platform is restricted to active UIU students and staff.
