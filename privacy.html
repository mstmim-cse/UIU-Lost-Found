Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect your name and UIU email address when you register. When you submit a lost or found post, we collect the item description, location, and any uploaded images.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to facilitate the return of lost items within the United International University community. We do not share your data with third parties.

3. Contact Information Protection

Your personal contact details (phone number, email) are kept hidden from other users until you explicitly accept a claim request.
